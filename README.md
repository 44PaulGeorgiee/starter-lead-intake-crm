# Lead Intake CRM Starter

## Overview

A lightweight CRM built using Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail, and Apps Script.

The platform has evolved from a basic lead capture tool into a complete sales pipeline management system designed to help solo founders and small businesses manage customer acquisition without paying for expensive CRM software.

---

## Problem Statement

As a solo founder, I need a reliable way to track and manage potential clients, but investing in expensive CRM software doesn't make sense before the business is generating consistent revenue.

Without a centralized system, leads can easily be forgotten, follow-ups can be delayed, and valuable opportunities can be lost. Managing client inquiries across emails, spreadsheets, and notes creates unnecessary friction and makes it difficult to measure progress in client acquisition.

As the number of leads grows, another challenge emerges:

```text
Which leads are progressing?

Which opportunities are most valuable?

How much potential revenue is in the pipeline?

Which clients are close to closing?
```

Without a sales pipeline, answering these questions becomes increasingly difficult.

---

## Solution

To solve this problem, I built a lightweight CRM using Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail, and Google Apps Script.

The system automatically:

- Captures leads
- Generates unique Lead IDs
- Sends Welcome Emails
- Tracks Lead Lifecycle Status
- Schedules Follow-Ups
- Scores and Prioritizes Leads
- Tracks Opportunities
- Calculates Pipeline Value
- Monitors Lead Progress through the Sales Process

This allows me to manage the entire lead acquisition and sales process without the cost of commercial CRM platforms while building the operational foundations of my business.

---

## Business Objective

The goal of this project is to create a low-cost client acquisition system that helps me:

- Capture every lead consistently
- Track the progress of each prospect
- Improve response times
- Standardize client communication
- Monitor follow-up activities
- Prioritize high-value opportunities
- Forecast potential revenue
- Build a repeatable sales process
- Validate business demand before investing in paid CRM software

# Sales Pipeline

The CRM now supports the complete sales lifecycle.

### New Lead

Lead enters the CRM.

### Contacted

Initial outreach has been completed.

### Qualified

Lead meets business requirements and opportunity criteria.

### Discovery Call

A consultation or discovery meeting has been scheduled.

### Proposal Sent

A proposal or service offer has been presented.

### Negotiation

Pricing, scope, or terms are being discussed.

### Won

Lead became a client.

### Lost

Lead did not convert.
