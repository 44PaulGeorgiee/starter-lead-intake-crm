Lead Intake CRM Starter
Overview
A lightweight CRM built using Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail, and Apps Script.

Problem Statement

As a solo founder, I need a reliable way to track and manage potential clients, but investing in expensive CRM software doesn't make sense before the business is generating consistent revenue.

Without a centralized system, leads can easily be forgotten, follow-ups can be delayed, and valuable opportunities can be lost. Managing client inquiries across emails, spreadsheets, and notes creates unnecessary friction and makes it difficult to measure progress in client acquisition.

Solution

To solve this problem, I built a lightweight CRM using Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail, and Google Apps Script.

The system automatically captures leads from an intake form, stores them in a centralized CRM database, assigns unique lead IDs, tracks lead status, schedules follow-up dates, and sends automated welcome emails.

This allows me to manage the entire lead intake process without the cost of commercial CRM platforms while building the operational foundations of my business.

Business Objective

The goal of this project is to create a low-cost client acquisition system that helps me:

Capture every lead consistently
Track the progress of each prospect
Improve response times
Standardize client communication
Monitor follow-up activities
Build a repeatable sales process
Validate business demand before investing in paid CRM software

This creates:

Slow response times

Poor lead visibility
Scattered customer information
